# Upper Tamakoshi Hydropower Project

**Upper Tamakoshi** is a 456 MW run-of-river hydropower plant on the Tama Koshi River in Dolakha district, Nepal. It is owned and operated by Upper Tamakoshi Hydropower Limited (UTKHPL), a company established by the Nepal Electricity Authority (NEA). It began commercial operation in 2021 and is the largest operating hydropower plant in Nepal. Financial distress at the company has been reported since a September 2024 flood; the evidence for this is graded below.

*Status as of 10 July 2026: operating. Figures marked "secondary" have not been checked against a company filing.*

## Operations and output

The plant is recorded as operating in the April and May 2026 company filings.

**Generation, secondary evidence only.** A research compilation reports the figures below. No company filing in the wiki confirms them.

| Fiscal year | Generation | Share of 2,281 GWh |

| FY 2079/80 | 1,945.83 GWh | 85.3% |
| FY 2080/81 | 2,058.36 GWh | 90.2% |
| FY 2081/82 | 1,529.07 GWh | 67.0% |

The compilation attributes the FY 2081/82 figure to an 88-day shutdown after the 2024 flood. The compilation is unverified; see [[ukhl-financials-generation-fy2079-82]].

## Finances

**Primary figures ([[utkhpl-disclosures-2026]]).**
- FY 2080/81 electricity-sales revenue: NPR 8.679 billion.
- FY 2080/81 insurance expense: NPR 99.3 million, about 1.14% of that revenue. This is one project-year, not a sector figure.
- Quarter ending Chaitra 2082 (unaudited): revenue NPR 7.718 billion, finance costs NPR 4.052 billion, net profit NPR 286.8 million. The source calls this a "quarter". The revenue figure is large relative to the annual figure, so it may be cumulative. The wiki does not resolve this.

**Secondary figures only ([[ukhl-financials-generation-fy2079-82]]).**
- Debt-to-equity: 75:25 was the original financing model. 88:12 is the effective ratio at commissioning, after capitalised interest during construction.
- Rating: ICRA Nepal "D" (default), attributed to FY 2081/82.
- Paid-up capital doubled from NPR 10.59 billion to NPR 21.18 billion through a 100% right share.
- Net losses and total debt (about NPR 78.6 billion) appear in the existing wiki page. No primary filing in the wiki confirms them.

## 2024 flood and insurance

Post-monsoon flooding in late September 2024 (Ashwin 2081) damaged surface structures, per the secondary compilation. The compilation estimates physical damage at NPR 1.79 billion and business-interruption loss at NPR 1.43 billion. It gives the insurance claim as NPR 2.0 billion, with a range of NPR 2.0–3.22 billion elsewhere.

The primary filing says property and lost-revenue claims were lodged and were still in settlement processing at the report date. It gives no amount paid and no later outcome. Statements in the existing wiki page about coverage exclusions and reinsurer objections have no linked source and are not repeated here. Market context is in [[hydro-insurance]].

## Expansion: Rolwaling diversion

**What the filings show.** The annual statements describe a 22 MW Rolwaling Khola project with 105.04 GWh of additional generation. They say a diversion is expected to add 216.51 GWh to Upper Tamakoshi. They record an EPC award, a PPA application in process, and construction ongoing at the report date. The May 2026 quarter shows NPR 2.103 billion of Rolwaling capital work in progress.

**What they do not show.** They give no percentage complete, no stop or restart date, no commissioning date and no operating contribution. A capital balance alone does not establish progress. The compilation's 212 GWh and its "4 to 6 hour" peaking claim are not in the filings. Status is unverified.
