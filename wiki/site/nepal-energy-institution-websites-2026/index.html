Summary

This record lists the official homepages and "about" pages of Nepal's core energy institutions, as read on 24 November 2026. It supports the institution entity pages and data/energy_institutions.csv. Each line gives only what the page itself states. Where a page did not state something, such as an establishment date or governing act, the fact is recorded as not stated.

Key Findings

Institution URL read What the page states
MoEWRI https://moewri.gov.np/ ; /pages/introduction-to-the-ministry/ Ministry of Energy, Water Resources and Irrigation. Lists WECS, DWRI, DoED, NEA, ERC, AEPC, VUCL, RPGCL, HIDCL and NNRFC under important links. States that the ministry was formed by merging the former Ministries of Energy and Irrigation, with AEPC and DHM moved in from the former environment ministry. Functions are cited to the Allocation of Business Rules 2083. No formation date is given.
ERC https://www.erc.gov.np/ ; /introduction-to-commission Electricity Regulatory Commission. Established 2076 Baisakh 23 BS under the Electricity Regulatory Commission Act 2074 and Regulations 2075. Describes itself as an autonomous, independent regulator of electricity tariffs and consumer interests. Publishes a consumer tariff-fixation directive (2083), tariff orders, directives, annual reports, and lists of PPA-consent and share-issue applications.
DoED https://www.doed.gov.np/ ; /pages/introduction Established as the Electricity Development Center on 16 July 1993 (2050 Shrawan 1) and renamed Department of Electricity Development on 7 February 2000 (2056 Magh 24). Sits under MoEWRI. Provides a "One Window" licensing service. Publishes survey and generation licence lists, application lists, power-plant lists by technology and cancelled-licence lists. No governing act is named on the page.
RPGCL https://rpgcl.com/ Rastriya Prasaran Grid Company Limited. "Established by the Government of Nepal on 12 July 2015" to "transmit and evacuate the power". Promoter shareholders are MOF, MOEWRI, other ministries and NEA, with no percentages given. Lists 11 transmission-line projects and an objective of transmission capacity to wheel 40 GW by 2040.
HIDCL https://www.hidcl.org.np/ Hydroelectricity Investment and Development Company Limited. Established 11 July 2011 under a Government of Nepal decision. Promoters and shareholders are MoF, MoEWRI, the Ministry of Law, FCGO, EPF, Rastriya Beema Sansthan and CIT. Publishes an FY 2081/82 annual report.
VUCL https://vucl.org/ Vidhyut Utpadan Company Limited. "Formally established on 20 November 2016". Promoter shareholders include MOEWRI, MOF, NEA and HIDCL. Lists Phukot Karnali PROR (480 MW), Kimathanka Arun (454 MW), Mugu Karnali Storage (1,902 MW) and Dullu Malika Solar (30 MW).
IBN https://ibn.gov.np/ ; /pages/introduction Office of the Investment Board. A high-level government body chaired by the Prime Minister, which promotes and manages domestic and foreign investment. No establishment date, governing act or project-size threshold is stated.
IPPAN https://www.ippan.org.np/ Independent Power Producers' Association, Nepal. Established 2001 as a non-profit, non-government autonomous organisation, with 442 corporate and 18 associate members. Publishes Energy Statistics of Nepal 2026 and newsletters.
SEBON https://www.sebon.gov.np/ Securities Board of Nepal. Publishes IPO, FPO and rights-share approvals, an IPO pipeline, registered securities and annual reports. No establishment date or governing act is stated on the homepage.
DHM https://www.dhm.gov.np/ The homepage header reads "Nepal Meteorological Department" under MoEWRI. Publishes rainfall, river-level and real-time stream-flow observations and has a data-request form.
NOC https://noc.org.np/ ; https://www.noc.org.np/ Blocked. Both URLs returned HTTP 403 on 24 November 2026. No content was read.

Limitations

  • These are website statements on a single date. They are not statutes, and they do not verify statutory mandates, ownership percentages or current office-holders.
  • Several homepages omit establishment dates and governing acts. Those fields stay unknown in the registry rather than being filled from memory.
  • The NOC website could not be read, so NOC facts in the wiki come from WECS Energy Sector Synopsis Report 2024.
  • Site structure and wording change frequently; re-verify before relying on a specific phrase.
