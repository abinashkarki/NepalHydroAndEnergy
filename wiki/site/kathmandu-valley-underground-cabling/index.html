Summary

Kathmandu Valley Underground Cabling is NEA's urban distribution-modernization programme for parts of the capital's medium- and low-voltage network. The page records the programme as presented in the FY 2019/20 annual book, including ceremonial and cable-laying evidence.

Source note

nea-transmission-annual-book-2077 The annual book shows the program both as a ceremonial milestone on the opening pages and again in the project-management chapter, where cable-laying works in Chabahil are presented as part of the Kathmandu Valley distribution enhancement package.

Why It Matters

  • It is one of the clearest examples of NEA trying to modernize the urban grid, not just add generation or long-distance transmission.
  • It links directly to Nepal Electricity Authority (NEA)'s distribution-reliability mandate and to the broader network-upgrade frame captured in Nepal Transmission System — 400 kV Landscape & Cross-Border Plan (2025).

Limitations & Controversies

The current page is based on annual-book evidence of the programme and does not provide a full package-by-package implementation record. Coverage area, completion status, outage impact, cost, contractor performance, and current operational benefits need newer distribution-project records before detailed evaluation.
