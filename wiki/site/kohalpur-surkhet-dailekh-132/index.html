Summary

The Kohalpur-Surkhet-Dailekh 132 kV grid reach is an under-construction western transmission extension into Karnali. The page records its route-length basis, named anchors, and role in showing the western hills as grid-reach territory rather than fully disconnected terrain.

Map Interpretation

This line is the key Karnali grid-reach story. It shows why the map treats the western hills as grid-reach territory while still showing the corridor as under construction rather than operational.

Current validation result: 1 feature, 0 inferred connectors, 0 remaining endpoint gaps, and 76.144 km traced length against an 84.0 route-km basis. Public decision: default-visible, caveated.

Limitations & Controversies

The public geometry is a route-level reconstruction through named anchors, not a tower alignment. NEA reports 52 km from Kohalpur to Surkhet and 32 km from Surkhet to Dailekh, with construction progress uneven across the two sections.
