Summary

ICRA's 29 June 2026 rating rationale for GMR Energy Limited. The report includes a current description of GMR Upper Karnali Hydropower Limited, its proposed partner structure and its development stage.

Key Findings

  • Reported ownership structure: GMR 34%, SJVN 34%, IREDA 5% and NEA 27%.
  • The report describes Upper Karnali as being at a very early stage of development.
  • It records approximately INR 112.5 crore of debt associated with the project and states that servicing requires promoter support.
  • It says partner funding is expected through a certain development stage; it does not state that full project financial close has been achieved.

Relevance

This is the current ownership and stage source for Upper Karnali. It supports separating partner participation and interim funding from financial close, disbursement and construction commencement.

Limitations

The document is a credit-rating rationale for GMR Energy, not the project company's financing agreement, executed PPA, lender closing notice or construction report. It does not resolve the current PDA, court or offtake position.
