Summary

This 19 November 2026 report covers India's Central Electricity Authority approval, granted on 18 November 2026, for NEA to sell 594 MW on the Indian Energy Exchange. It also reports an additional approval for the Seti Khola project.

Key Claims

  • The 594 MW IEX approval runs from 18 November 2026 to 14 June 2027 (Mangsir 2, 2083 to Jestha 31, 2084). Of this, 389 MW goes through NVVN on the 400 kV Dhalkebar-Muzaffarpur line and 205 MW through PTC on the 132 kV Kataiya-Kusaha, Raxaul-Parwanipur and Ramnagar-Surajpur lines.
  • Seti Khola received an additional approval to export 21.34 MW, valid to 31 August 2027 (Bhadra 15, 2084).
  • The report puts the total authorised export capacity from Nepal to India at 1,198 MW.

Relevance

This report is the source for the Seti Khola FY 2026/27 plant-approval row in Cross-Border Export Approvals Ledger.

Limitations

  • It does not split the 594 MW by plant.
