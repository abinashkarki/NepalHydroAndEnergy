Summary

Rastriya Prasaran Grid Company Limited (RPGCL) is a state-promoted transmission company. Its website says the Government of Nepal established it on 12 July 2015 "to transmit and evacuate the power for the development and operation of the hydropower sector."

Role in Nepal's Energy System

RPGCL develops transmission corridors alongside Nepal Electricity Authority (NEA). Its website lists 11 transmission-line projects:

  • Haitar–Sitalpati (Arun Corridor) 400 kV
  • Bheri Corridor 400 kV
  • Humla–Phukot 400 kV
  • Phukot–Karmadev
  • West Seti–Dododhara
  • Tamor–Dhungesanghu 220 kV
  • Sitalpati–Dhungesanghu 220 kV
  • Lamabagar–Barhabise 220 kV
  • Mewa–Change 132 kV
  • Kerabari–New Marsyangdi 132 kV
  • Sanfebagar–Basti–Betan 132 kV

NEA's FY 2025/26 annual report says a separate SPV has been set up to take over the West Seti 400 kV transmission line from RPGCL and build it under a PPP model (PDF p.115).

Limitations & Controversies

The website does not publish ownership percentages, project costs or completion status on its homepage. This page does not verify the progress of any listed line.
