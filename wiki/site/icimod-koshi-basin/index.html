Summary

ICIMOD's research programme on the Koshi Basin, covering downstream impacts, agricultural dependency, and flood dynamics. Supports the geopolitical significance of Nepal's eastern river system.

Key Data Points

  • Koshi basin supports >40 million people
  • Contributes disproportionately high sediment loads to the Ganges system
  • Koshi irrigates ~500,000–600,000 ha in Bihar
  • The Koshi is known as the "River of Sorrow" in Bihar due to shifting course and catastrophic flooding

Relevance to Project

Central to Hydro-Geopolitics and Nepal's Contribution to the Ganges. Also relevant to Sediment as Design Constraint given extreme sediment loads. .

Limitations

  • Programme page, not a single technical report. The source summarizes ICIMOD's Koshi Basin Programme rather than providing one citable methods document.
  • Directional sediment framing. The page supports high-level sediment and flood-risk context but does not provide design-grade suspended-sediment parameters.
  • Downstream aggregate scale. Population and irrigation figures are basin-scale context; they do not allocate impacts by project, district, or season.
  • Programme date ambiguity. The page uses a 2023 source date, but the programme spans multiple outputs with different publication dates.
