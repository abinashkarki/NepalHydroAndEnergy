NEA Standard PPA Rate Schedule

NEA's April 2017 posted power-purchase rates for RoR, PRoR and reservoir hydropower, the 2024 solar competitive-award tariff range, and NEA's FY 2025/26 average purchase rate, with explicit gaps for wind, biomass and a solar ceiling.

Caveat

The hydropower rates are the 27 April 2017 NEA Board schedule as reproduced in an ERC-commissioned discussion paper; the paper does not say whether the RoR and PRoR rates remain unchanged today, and it states that the reservoir rates were withdrawn.

Summary

ppa_standard_rates.csv has 25 rows: 16 RoR/PRoR rates and 2 withdrawn reservoir rates from the NEA Board schedule effective 2074/01/14 BS (27 April 2017), 3 solar rows summarising the 2024 competitive award, 3 explicit gap rows (solar ceiling, wind, biomass) and 1 context row for NEA's reported average purchase rate.

Finding

In the 2017 schedule, every RoR and PRoR wet-season rate and every PRoR dry-season non-peak rate is NRs 4.80/kWh. The PRoR rates differ only in the dry-season peak rate, which rises from NRs 8.50 (1–2 peak hours) to NRs 10.55 (4–6 hours).

Finding

The 2024 solar award tariffs (NRs 4.99–5.54/kWh, single rate) are higher than the 2017 hydropower wet-season rate (NRs 4.80) and lower than its dry-season rate (NRs 8.40).

Coverage / Method

  • Hydropower rows come from Table 1 of ERC/ECA Discussion Paper on Storage Hydro PPA Pricing (2025) (printed p.9, PDF p.14). The paper cites "NEA Board Decisions on the Power Purchase Rates and Associated Rules for PPA of ROR/PROR/Storage Projects Effective from 2074/01/14". The season definitions are from the same table: wet is 16 Jestha–15 Mangsir and dry is 16 Mangsir–15 Jestha.
  • The source has PRoR bands of 1–2, 2–3, 3–4 and 4–6 daily peak hours at rated capacity. The CSV keeps these bands instead of the runbook's planned 1–2 / 2–4 / 4–6 split.
  • Solar rows aggregate the 63 award rows of NEA Notice of Intent to Award Solar Projects (2024), as transcribed in data/solar_project_specs.csv. The capacity-weighted mean is computed here.
  • The average purchase rate is from the FY 2025/26 key financial indicators table in NEA Annual Report FY 2025/26 (PDF p.158). The table marks the figure provisional, subject to audit.

Caveats

  • Escalation: the paper states the 3%-for-8-years escalator only for reservoir rates. For RoR and PRoR the CSV records "not stated in source". Project-level escalation terms reported in ratings documents are on PPA Pricing and are not used here.
  • Tenure and take-or-pay: these are unknown for every row. No official NEA or ERC text on disk states them; the secondary legal analysis in NEA Standard Hydropower PPA Clauses — AD Penalty, Reserve Margin, and Curtailment is not used as a rate source.
  • Gaps: no official posted rate was verified for a solar ceiling, wind or biomass. These rows are kept as explicit unknown gaps.
  • Reservoir: the current reservoir framework is ERC Reservoir-Based Generating Station Electricity Purchase and Sale Directive 2082, which sets a tariff methodology, not a posted rate.
  • Signed PPAs: individual signed PPAs may differ from the posted schedule.
