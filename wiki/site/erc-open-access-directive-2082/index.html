Summary

Current-status source for Nepal's Open Access Directive 2082. Public reporting in January 2026 states that the Electricity Regulatory Commission issued the directive for open access to the transmission and distribution system.

Key Data Points

  • ERC issued the Open Access Directive 2082 in January 2026.
  • The directive opens a legal route for eligible generators and large consumers to use the grid on a non-discriminatory open-access basis.
  • Public reporting still treats implementation detail as a next step; "issued" is safer than "fully operationalized."

Relevance

Updates older wiki language that treated open access as only a draft or consultation policy. The directive changes the legal status of the open-access reform leg, while leaving broader ISO creation and AD penalty separation unresolved.

Limitations

  • This page uses public reporting as the accessible status source. The directive text and any implementing manuals should be added when a stable official ERC URL is archived.
  • Issuance does not prove effective operational implementation by NEA, grid users, or market participants.
