Summary

The Independent Power Producers' Association, Nepal (IPPAN) is the industry association of Nepal's private power producers. Its website says it was established in 2001 "with the intention of encouraging the private sector" and describes it as a non-profit, non-government autonomous organisation.

Role in Nepal's Energy System

IPPAN represents independent power producers, who sell to Nepal Electricity Authority (NEA) under PPAs. Its homepage republishes NEA-sourced capacity figures, labelled "NEA / Bhadra 2083": 4,161.827 MW in total, of which 661.10 MW is NEA and 3,500.727 MW is IPP. These are an association's display of NEA data, not a primary NEA record. NEA Annual Report FY 2025/26 remains the official capacity source.

WECS lists IPPAN among the institutions it collects electricity data from for its energy synopsis (WECS Energy Sector Synopsis Report 2024, Table 3.1). IPPAN office-holders also appear as named sources in news coverage, for example on the August 2026 Bhotekoshi flood losses (Kathmandu Post — Flood damage to Nepal's hydropower sector could run into billions (2026)).

Limitations & Controversies

IPPAN is an interest group. Its statements and loss estimates are advocacy-adjacent and are not engineering or regulatory findings. Its republished capacity figures can differ in date and scope from NEA's annual report.
