Summary

Official Nepal Electricity Authority annual-book publication for FY 2019/20 (B.S. 2077). The volume mixes directorate narrative, project status notes, site/substation photographs, appendix tables, and map pages. For the wiki, its highest-value material is in the transmission chapter and appendix: project-by-project line/substation status, existing substation capacity, and under-construction high-voltage grid inventory.

Relevance to Project

This is the primary official source for NEA's mid-2020 view of the transmission system. It is especially useful for:

  • Hetauda–Dhalkebar–Inaruwa 400 kV Backbone stage/status details
  • Dhalkebar–Muzaffarpur 400 kV Cross-Border Interconnection substation and energization context
  • appendix-level substation capacity and under-construction inventory pages
  • project/program pages that need official inline figures rather than generic district scenery

Local Source

Local workspace copy: nea_transmission_annual_book_2077.pdf

Limitations

  • FY 2019/20 snapshot: Post-2020 commissioning outcomes for projects listed as under-construction are not covered.
  • Superseded for totals: Later FY 2024/25 system totals in NEA Annual Report FY 2024/25 replace the aggregate figures here.
  • No technical modelling: Does not contain detailed load-flow or dispatch modelling.
  • Mixed genre: Directorates narrative, project status notes, and appendix tables may include promotional framing alongside raw data.

What This Source Does Not Cover

  • post-2020 commissioning outcomes for projects still under construction here
  • later FY 2024/25 system totals already superseded in NEA Annual Report FY 2024/25
  • detailed load-flow or dispatch modelling
