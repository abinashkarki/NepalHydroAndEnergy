Summary

Reference compilation for the 140 MW Tanahu Hydropower Project's construction progress and social-compliance posture, drawn from ADB's own project monitoring reports, two Accountability Mechanism complaints (2024 and 2025), and a later public construction-progress update.

Key Data Points

  • Installed: 140 MW reservoir-type plant
  • River: Seti (Gandaki basin)
  • District: Tanahu
  • Owner: Nepal Electricity Authority
  • Financier: Asian Development Bank (primary), co-financiers
  • Main civil works: 140 m high roller-compacted-concrete dam (Package-1), underground powerhouse (Package-2), main water conveyance tunnel
  • Status (FY 2024/25): ~67% complete per NEA annual report
  • Latest public progress check (April 2026): roughly 75% complete per Tanahu Hydropower Progress Update (April 2026)
  • ADB monitoring visit: December 2024 — flagged buffer-zone demarcation, public-land acquisition and construction pace as open issues

Accountability Mechanism complaints

  • 2024 complaint: demarcation of buffer zones, free prior informed consent, land-for-land compensation, untitled-land registration
  • 2025 complaint: filed 18 Feb 2025, deemed eligible for review 4 Apr 2025

Relevance to Project

Primary source for Tanahu's status, design basics and the unusually well-documented compliance issues that shape its completion schedule. The Accountability Mechanism filings are the reason Tanahu's delays are public and auditable — most Nepali storage projects lack that transparency layer.

What This Source Does Not Cover

  • Dry-season energy contribution once the reservoir is in operation
  • Plant-level cost escalation numbers (ADB figures redact subsidiary contractor detail)
  • Coordination with downstream irrigation users on the Seti

Limitations

  • Compilation, not primary project documents. Drawn from ADB monitoring reports and Accountability Mechanism filings — does not include the full PAD, EIA, or contractor progress reports.
  • Construction progress is time-sensitive. The ~67% completion figure is from the NEA FY 2024/25 annual report; the ~75% figure is a later public update and should be refreshed before final publication or narration lock.
  • Accountability Mechanism complaints are unresolved. Two complaints (2024 and 2025) are filed and eligible for review but not yet adjudicated. Their resolution may affect project timeline and cost.
