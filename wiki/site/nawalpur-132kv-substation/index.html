Summary

Nawalpur 132 kV Substation is a planned 132/33 kV, 63 MVA node intended to improve local supply reliability in Nawalpur through new substation and 33 kV sub-transmission works.

A planned 132/33 kV substation project intended to improve supply reliability in Nawalpur and connect back into the Haripur 33/11 kV node through new 33 kV sub-transmission links.

Source note

nea-transmission-annual-book-2077 Book page 54 describes the Nawalpur substation as a 132/33 kV, 63 MVA node with additional 33 kV double-circuit line works to feed the local load area.

Why It Matters

  • This is the kind of mid-voltage reinforcement asset that sits between the headline 220/400 kV corridors and the distribution-level service outcome.
  • It adds detail to the broader story of Nepal Electricity Authority (NEA) trying to convert bulk power growth into local reliability improvements.

Limitations & Controversies

The page is based on the FY 2019/20 NEA annual-book description and does not establish the current construction status, final equipment configuration, commissioning date, or measured reliability effect after completion.
