Caveat

Not found in the copied public corpus does not prove that an institution does not collect the measure internally.

Summary

The register records missing public measures, why they matter and the next evidence required. It currently covers interruption and voltage quality, feeder hosting capacity, hourly demand, cooking adoption, batteries, reserves, ancillary services, dispatch and state of charge.

What This Shows

The register keeps those missing measures visible instead of replacing them with invented coverage.

Coverage / Method

The electricity_system_data_gaps.csv records each measure's branch, current public status, reason for inclusion, next evidence needed and review date.

Caveats

“Not found in the public corpus” is an evidence-status statement, not a claim that the data do not exist anywhere.
