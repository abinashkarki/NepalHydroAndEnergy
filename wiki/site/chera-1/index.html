Summary

Chera-1 is a 149 MW planning-stage storage candidate in the Karnali basin. The page records its JICA/NEA shortlist metrics, including annual energy, dry-season energy, and storage volume, for comparison with other storage candidates.

Why It Matters

Chera-1 offers 563.2 GWh of annual energy with 120.6 GWh of dry-season output (21.4% dry share) — a meaningful contribution to Karnali-basin firming, though its storage volumes are more modest than the larger shortlist candidates on the Karnali main stem.

Status And Timing

Chera-1 should be read as a planning / promising-storage candidate surfacing from the JICA/NEA storage master plan. The IPSDP milestone ladder may place it in a later windows (2031–2040). There is no confirmed construction start in the current wiki evidence set.

Limitations & Controversies

The map marker is a river-aligned project reference. Use it for basin-level and strategic comparison, not for final dam-axis coordinates.

The current evidence supports planning-stage comparison, not a confirmed construction programme. Design maturity, licensing status, financing, social/environmental review, final location, and COD timing require source-level verification before project-specific analysis.
