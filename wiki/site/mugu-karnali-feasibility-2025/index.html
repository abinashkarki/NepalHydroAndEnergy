Summary

Reference compilation for the 1,902 MW Mugu Karnali Storage Hydroelectric Project — Nepal's largest proposed generation asset, in pre-feasibility under Vidhyut Utpadan Company Limited (VUC) and open to public–private partnership bidding.

Key Data Points

  • Capacity: 1,902 MW
  • Type: Storage / pumped-storage; 10 Francis turbines
  • Gross head: ~285 m
  • Design annual energy: ~5,883 GWh
  • River: Karnali
  • District: Mugu (registry); Bajura also referenced in early materials
  • Developer: Vidhyut Utpadan Company Limited (VUCL / VUC) — state-owned
  • Structure: PPP, VUC seeking 51% partnership stake
  • Survey licence: granted by DoED; 2-year feasibility deadline with extension possible to 5 years
  • Expressions of interest (2024–2025):
    • Adani Group — EOI (no formal bid as of January 2025)
    • Chowdhury Group (CG Energo Pvt Ltd) — formal bid submitted, only competitor on record as of January 2025
  • Planned schedule (Power Technology): construction from ~2030, COD ~2034

Relevance to Project

Primary source for Mugu Karnali's scale, schedule, bidding posture and the India-vs-Bangladesh developer interest that makes it politically visible. Notable because 1,902 MW is larger than Nepal's entire current operating hydropower fleet.

Limitations

  • Pre-feasibility stage: The project is at pre-feasibility; no detailed hydrology, sediment, or seismic study outcomes are published.
  • Transmission gap: A project of this scale implies new 400 kV or higher corridor infrastructure that does not yet exist; no evacuation plan is disclosed.
  • Environmental/social scoping absent: No full EIA has been disclosed; the project is at the expression-of-interest stage.
  • Bid landscape fluid: Only one formal bid (Chowdhury Group) is on record as of January 2025; the competitive landscape may shift.
  • Schedule aspirational: The 2030 construction / 2034 COD timeline is a Power Technology projection, not a committed project schedule.
