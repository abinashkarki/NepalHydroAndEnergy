Summary

Mithila Solar is a 10 MWp operating utility solar plant in Dhanusha district, Madhesh Province, developed by Eco Power Development Company Pvt. Ltd. Commissioned circa 2021, it is the largest single Phase I IPP solar plant and a grid-adjacent Terai siting archetype. The page tracks it as a Phase I operational benchmark using the NEA annual report source set.

Why it matters

Mithila Solar's siting is the clearest Phase I validation of the grid-adjacent Terai siting archetype later encoded in the NEA 960 MW National Solar Tender:

  • Mithila / Janakpur is the heart of Nepal's densest 132 kV grid cluster (Hetauda–Dhalkebar–Inaruwa 400 kV Backbone).
  • Dhanusha has Zone B2 GHI of ~4.9–5.1 kWh/m²/day — among the best Terai solar resource.
  • Flat agricultural terrain with <2° slope.
  • Proximity (within ~70 km) to the Dhalkebar–Muzaffarpur 400 kV cross-border interconnection (Dhalkebar–Muzaffarpur 400 kV Cross-Border Interconnection).

Mithila Solar is the pattern match for dozens of 960 MW tender awards in Dhanusha, Mahottari, Sarlahi, and Bara; its operational track record is the evidence base IPPs used when bidding the Phase IV tender at near-LCOE prices.

Limitations & Controversies

The current page is strongest on programme and siting-context from NEA annual report evidence. It does not independently verify exact commissioning date, current monthly generation, availability, degradation rate, land tenure details, or O&M arrangements beyond the existing source set. The ~16 GWh/yr expected generation figure is a planning estimate, not a verified operational output. Phase I PPA tariff is approximate.
