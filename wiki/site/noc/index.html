Summary

Nepal Oil Corporation (NOC) is the state petroleum supplier. The WECS Energy Sector Synopsis 2024 describes the petroleum sector as "operated by the Nepal Oil Corporation". It uses NOC as the source for petroleum import, sales and storage data.

Role in Nepal's Energy System

The WECS synopsis states that Nepal imports petroleum products mainly through its border with India. Table 4.4 lists 16 storage locations, including Amlekhgunj, Biratnagar, Bhairahawa, Nepalgunj, Pokhara and Dhangadi. Line-level storage and pipeline detail is in Load Anchors and Fossil Backbone Registry.

The Embassy of India's 2019 release describes the Motihari–Amlekhgunj petroleum products pipeline: 69 km, 10-inch diameter and 2 MMTPA capacity. Indian Oil Corporation built it in collaboration with NOC (Embassy of India Release on the Motihari–Amlekhgunj Pipeline (2019)).

Key Datasets & Disclosures

  • Petroleum import and sales statistics, cited by WECS as "NOC, 2023. Petroleum Import and Sales Statistics"
  • Storage capacity by location, as reproduced by WECS

WECS notes that NOC lacks LPG sales data for some provinces because LPG depots handle those sales.

Limitations & Controversies

All figures here are second-hand, through WECS. Table 4.4 has no diesel (HSD) column and states no unit. The WECS import text gives petrol and diesel imports in "kL" at magnitudes that look like thousand-kL values. NOC's own website could not be read, so this page records no establishment date or ownership. The pipeline figures come from the Indian side's release.
