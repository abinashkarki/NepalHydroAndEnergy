Nepal Energy Institutions Registry

A 14-row registry of Nepal's core energy institutions, recording each one's establishment date, statutory basis, parent body, mandate, website and public datasets, with unknowns left explicit.

Caveat

Most rows rest on the institutions' own websites as read on 24 November 2026; where a site did not state a date or act, the field is `unknown` rather than filled from memory.

Summary

energy_institutions.csv lists 14 institutions:

  • ministry and departments: Ministry of Energy, Water Resources and Irrigation (MoEWRI), Department of Electricity Development (DoED), DHM
  • planning and promotion bodies: Water and Energy Commission Secretariat (WECS), Alternative Energy Promotion Centre (AEPC)
  • utility: Nepal Electricity Authority (NEA)
  • regulators: Electricity Regulatory Commission (ERC), SEBON
  • state-promoted companies: Rastriya Prasaran Grid Company Limited (RPGCL), Hydroelectricity Investment and Development Company Limited (HIDCL), VUCL
  • investment board: Investment Board Nepal (IBN)
  • industry association: Independent Power Producers' Association, Nepal (IPPAN)
  • petroleum supplier: Nepal Oil Corporation (NOC)

Each row carries the source URL, locator and verification date.

Finding

Three of the state-promoted energy companies publish their founding as a Government of Nepal decision or establishment date and do not name an enabling act on their homepages: HIDCL (11 July 2011), RPGCL (12 July 2015) and VUCL (20 November 2016).

Finding

NEA is listed as a promoter shareholder of both RPGCL and VUCL, and HIDCL is listed as a promoter shareholder of VUCL.

Coverage / Method

  • The rows come from the official-website review recorded in Nepal Energy Institution Official Websites (Nov 2026 Review).
  • NEA's incorporation date and act come from NEA Annual Report FY 2025/26 (PDF p.159). NOC's row uses WECS Energy Sector Synopsis Report 2024, because the NOC site returned HTTP 403.
  • AEPC's 1996 founding year comes from the existing Alternative Energy Promotion Centre (AEPC) page.
  • The runbook slug vucil is recorded as vucl, the company's own abbreviation.

Caveats

  • Website statements are not statutes, and ownership percentages are not published by the state-promoted companies.
  • The DHM homepage header reads "Nepal Meteorological Department". The registry keeps the DHM slug and does not resolve the naming change.
  • New entity pages were created for ERC, DoED, MoEWRI, RPGCL, HIDCL, IBN, IPPAN and NOC. No pages were created for VUCL, SEBON or DHM.
