Summary

The Nepal Electricity Authority Transmission Directorate's archived maintenance notices record local outages on 3 and 5 July 2026 associated with conductor-stringing work on the Hetauda–Dhalkebar section of the Hetauda–Dhalkebar–Duhabi 400 kV project.

Key Findings

  • The notices identify active conductor-stringing work in the Hetauda section in early July 2026.
  • They are operational outage notices, not a project completion report.
  • They do not state how many towers or spans remained, whether the entire section was strung, whether the line was energised, or what transfer capacity was available.

Relevance

This source bounds the current status used by Hetauda–Dhalkebar–Inaruwa 400 kV Backbone, Transmission and Delivery Are Material Immediate Monetization Constraints and Decision Dossier — Hetauda–Dhalkebar Completion and Community Resolution. It supports “construction activity continued in early July 2026,” not “the corridor was completed in July 2026.”

Limitations

  • Archive search results can change as NEA adds notices.
  • The notices concern temporary local shutdowns and do not provide a project milestone certificate.
  • No current primary-source tower inventory, energisation notice or commissioning certificate was located in the checked NEA public surfaces as of 19 July 2026.
