Summary

This data page records NEA's FY 2076/77 high-voltage transformer capacity and named under-construction substation queue from the Transmission Directorate annual-book appendix.

The annual book's appendix gives a rare structured view of both the installed high-voltage transformer base and the next wave of substation expansion under construction. Together, they show why NEA was still in a catch-up phase even as generation was accelerating.

Source note

nea-transmission-annual-book-2077 Appendix installed-capacity summary on book page 249 reports total existing high-voltage transformer capacity at 4299.7 MVA in FY 2076/77, up 364.4 MVA year over year.

Source note

nea-transmission-annual-book-2077 The same appendix's under-construction substation inventory totals 5550.5 MVA of additional high-voltage substation capacity in the active build queue.

Why It Matters

  • The under-construction queue was larger than the installed base, which is a direct signal that substation build-out was lagging the generation pipeline.
  • Several of the queue's biggest items sit on the same corridors already treated as strategic bottlenecks in Hetauda–Dhalkebar–Inaruwa 400 kV Backbone, Hetauda–Bharatpur–Bardaghat 220 kV Corridor, and Nepal Transmission System — 400 kV Landscape & Cross-Border Plan (2025).
  • The named queue also foreshadows later entity pages such as Nepal–India Electricity Transmission and Trade Project (NIETTP), Tingla 132/33 kV Substation, Dhungesanghu–Basantapur 220/132 kV Corridor, and New Modi–Lekhnath 132 kV Transmission Line.

Coverage / Method

The installed-capacity table and under-construction queue are drawn from the NEA Transmission Directorate annual-book appendix for FY 2076/77. The page preserves voltage ratios, transformer counts, MVA capacity, year-over-year increment where reported, project/substation names, and expected completion years from that source.

Caveats

The annual-book appendix is a historical snapshot and should not be read as current substation capacity. Under-construction entries are pipeline records from the source year, not confirmation that every substation was commissioned on the expected schedule or at the listed capacity.
