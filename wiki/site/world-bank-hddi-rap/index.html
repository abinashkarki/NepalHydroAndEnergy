Summary

Resettlement Action Plan for transmission components of the Nepal–India Electricity Transmission and Trade Project, including the Hetauda–Dhalkebar–Inaruwa 400 kV line. It documents route, right-of-way and affected-asset information.

Key Findings

  • The plan covers private land, structures, trees and other assets in the transmission right of way.
  • It provides district-level tables for affected structures and landowners along the Hetauda–Dhalkebar–Inaruwa alignment.
  • It distinguishes the transmission corridor from associated substations and other NIETTP components.
  • It is a route and social-impact source, not a current construction dashboard.

Relevance

This is the document-grounded route and right-of-way source for Hetauda–Dhalkebar–Inaruwa 400 kV Backbone.

Limitations

The plan predates the current segment status and does not establish the number of works remaining in July 2026. Current physical progress must come from NEA or project implementation reporting.
