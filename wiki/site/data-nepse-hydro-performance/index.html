Listed Hydropower Performance (Starter)

A strictly sourced starter table of delivered energy against design energy for public hydropower companies. It currently covers two NEA-linked companies (five company-years) and lists what is still missing for the rest of the listed sector.

Caveat

This is a two-company sample, not a sector census. Company-year rows appear only where NEA's annual report or a company filing states the figure. Net profit and total debt are blank because no primary annual figure was located on disk.

Summary

nepse_hydro_performance.csv records how much energy public hydropower companies actually delivered compared with their design or contracted energy, together with financial results where a primary document states them. NEA's annual report gives design energy for every project but does not itemise actual output for independent power producers, so company disclosures are needed to fill the gap.

The starter covers two companies and five company-years. Every value carries a page-level locator.

Finding

Upper Tamakoshi delivered more than its 2,281 GWh design energy for the first time in FY 2082/83 (2,388.02 GWh, 104.7%). The year before, which included the shutdown after the September 2024 flood, it delivered 67% of design.

Finding

From commercial operation on 6 October 2025 to the end of Ashadh 2083 (mid-July 2026), Middle Bhotekoshi beat its deemed energy target by 3.24%, according to NEA.

Method and Coverage

  • Delivered energy is NEA's published figure for energy delivered to NEA (NEA Annual Report FY 2025/26, PDF p.111 for Upper Tamakoshi and PDF p.117 for Middle Bhotekoshi). For FY 2081/82, NEA's previous report gives 1,529.07 GWh generated (PDF p.92); the table keeps the later report's delivered series and records the difference in the CSV notes.
  • Delivered vs. design is calculated as delivered ÷ design energy. For Middle Bhotekoshi, NEA states the percentage against a "deemed energy generation target" that it does not print, so the contracted column is left blank.
  • Revenue. FY 2080/81 comes from UTKHPL's annual statements (Upper Tamakoshi Hydropower Limited Disclosures (2026)). FY 2082/83 is NEA's rounded statement ("approximately 10.66 Arab"), not an audited figure.
  • Tickers are left blank because neither source states a NEPSE trading symbol.

Not Yet Covered

Primary figures were not found on disk for the following companies, and the public web results checked were unaudited quarterly summaries that disagree with each other, so they are excluded:

  • Chilime Hydropower Company — NEA's report confirms the NEPSE listing but gives no annual generation.
  • Sanjen Jalavidhyut Company — NEA gives only cumulative generation since COD (90.79 GWh and 96.96 GWh to Ashadh 2082), not annual figures.
  • Rasuwagadhi Hydropower Company — output was interrupted by flood damage, and NEA gives restart dates but no annual GWh.
  • Private listed IPPs (for example Sahas Urja, Sanima Mai, Butwal Power, Arun Valley) — need audited annual reports or rating rationales. The ICRA-based compilation in Mission 1: Sahas Urja (Solu Khola, 86 MW) — Benchmark IPP Success is a secondary research summary and has not been used here.

Limitations

  • Two companies are not a sample of the listed sector. Both are NEA-linked, so they may not represent private IPPs.
  • Delivered energy reflects grid curtailment and outages as well as hydrology.
  • Net profit and total debt are blank throughout. Quarterly unaudited results were not used.
