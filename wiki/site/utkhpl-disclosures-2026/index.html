Summary

UTKHPL's official project-update pages, third-quarter statement and annual reports, checked in July 2026. They provide a primary company filing trail for the operating 456 MW Upper Tamakoshi plant.

Key Findings

  • UTKHPL published its FY 2082/83 third-quarter financial report on 13 May 2026.
  • It published combined sixteenth and seventeenth annual reports for FY 2079/80 and FY 2080/81 on 6 April 2026.
  • The site identifies UTKHPL as the NEA-established implementing company and publishes company ownership information.
  • The FY 2082/83 third-quarter statement reports NPR 7.718 billion in electricity-sales revenue, NPR 4.052 billion in finance costs and NPR 286.8 million in net profit for the quarter ending Chaitra 2082.
  • The FY 2080/81 annual statements report NPR 8.679 billion in electricity-sales revenue and NPR 99.3 million in insurance expense. This is a single-project-year ratio of about 1.14%, not a sector-wide insurance-premium finding.
  • The annual statements describe a 22 MW Rolwaling Khola project with 105.04 GWh of additional generation and a diversion expected to add 216.51 GWh to Upper Tamakoshi. They record an EPC award, a PPA application in process and construction ongoing at the report date.
  • The May 2026 quarter reports NPR 2.103 billion of capital work in progress for Rolwaling. It does not provide a package completion percentage or a firm commissioning date.
  • The FY 2080/81 annual statements say that property and lost-revenue insurance claims had been lodged after the September 2024 flood and remained in settlement processing at the report date; they do not establish an amount paid or the claim's later status.

Relevance

This is the current company-disclosure source for Upper Tamakoshi. It should control when older research compilations conflict with newer financial or operating filings.

Limitations

The third-quarter statement is unaudited and published as a single image. The annual-report statements have an earlier reporting cut-off than the May 2026 quarter. Together they do not establish a current Rolwaling construction percentage, a stop/restart date, insurance settlement outcome, debt-restructuring agreement or reliable forward commissioning schedule.
