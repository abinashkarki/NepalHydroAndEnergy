Caveat

Summary

Nepal Digital Infrastructure Company Limited (NDICL) is a 100% subsidiary of the Nepal Electricity Authority, established on 9 March 2026. NEA says it was founded to support the Digital Nepal Framework by using NEA's physical assets (poles, transmission lines and rights-of-way) to build a national digital backbone. It works on two pillars: optical fibre infrastructure and data-centre services (NEA Annual Report FY 2025/26, PDF p.138, printed p.127).

Role in Nepal's Energy System

NDICL puts NEA's grid corridors to a second use. NEA presents the model as a new revenue stream for NEA, lower build-out costs for telecom operators that share utility infrastructure, and a secure framework for government data exchange. These are NEA's stated aims, not measured outcomes.

For siting energy-intensive digital loads, such as data centres, NDICL is the institution most likely to hold fibre routes along the transmission network. See Clean-Industry and Compute Siting Baseline for the grid and tariff facts that are public today.
