Evidence on Nepal’s electricity system

Nepal builds more hydropower every year, yet still imports electricity every winter. This wiki explains why, page by page, with every figure traced to its source and every claim given a confidence rating.

Start here · Read the argument · State of the system · Explore the map

Why does Nepal import power in winter?

Most plants run with the river and there is very little storage, so output falls with dry-season flows while demand does not.

  • Seasonal Mismatch
  • Storage Deficit
  • Operating Fleet Is Overwhelmingly RoR/PRoR, Not True Storage
  • Master Thesis

Who controls the grid?

The Nepal Electricity Authority dispatches the grid, buys most of the electricity and administers contract penalties, while also owning power plants that compete with the producers it judges.

  • NEA Triple-Authority Conflict
  • Decision Dossier — Power-System Role Separation and Open Access
