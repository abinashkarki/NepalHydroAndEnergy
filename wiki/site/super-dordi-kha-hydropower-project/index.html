Note

Disambiguation "Super" is the official Nepal DoED/MoE registry tier — distinct from "Upper". On the same Dordi river, Upper Dordi A HEP (25 MW) and Dordi Khola (27 MW) are separate projects. Super Dordi Kha (49.6 MW) is the largest plant on the Dordi. These are distinct assets with different promoters and PPAs.
