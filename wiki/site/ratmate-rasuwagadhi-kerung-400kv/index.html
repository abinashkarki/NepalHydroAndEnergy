Summary

Ratmate-Rasuwagadhi-Kerung is a proposed 400 kV Nepal-China interconnection concept linking Ratmate to the Rasuwagadhi/Kerung border axis.

Nepal's planned first grid interconnection with China in the annual-book 2020 planning frame. The project is both a power-trade idea and a geopolitical signal: a proposed 400 kV route from the Ratmate side of the Nepal grid to the Rasuwagadhi/Kerung border axis.

Source note

nea-transmission-annual-book-2077 Book page 65 says the Ratmate–Rasuwagadhi–Kerung line was being advanced as Nepal's first power-grid interconnection with China, with a planned ~70 km 400 kV double-circuit line from the Rasuwagadhi border point to Ratmate and a power-exchange substation at Ratmate.

Why It Matters

  • This is the clearest 2020-era public example of Nepal trying to widen its grid geopolitics beyond the India-only transmission frame.
  • Even as a survey-stage line, it belongs with Rasuwagadhi and Hydro-Geopolitics because the point of the project was energy-security optionality, not just another domestic corridor.

Limitations & Controversies

The available page evidence establishes the 2020 planning frame, route concept, and survey/feasibility status; it does not confirm a final alignment, implementation agreement, financing, construction start, or operating exchange arrangement with China.
