# Kathmandu Post / NDRRMA — Death toll from Bhotekoshi flood reaches 734 (2026)

## Summary

This 30 August 2026 report reproduces the NDRRMA 9 a.m. search, rescue and relief update for the 26 August 2026 flood: 734 bodies recovered, 2,498 people unaccounted for, and the district-by-district geography of the disaster — which together trace the flood's downstream route from Rasuwa to the Indian border.

## Key Claims

- 734 bodies including human remains recovered; **2,498 missing**, including 933 people linked to a hydropower project, 589 foreign nationals, 127 Nepalis travelling with them, and 85 security personnel.
- Bodies recovered hundreds of kilometres downstream along the Bhotekoshi and Trishuli: **Chitwan 259, Nawalparasi East 184, Nawalparasi West 82, Gorkha 58, Nuwakot 52, Dhading 50, Tanahun 36, Rasuwa 13**.
- Missing-person counts: Rasuwa 562, Nuwakot 115, Makawanpur 65.
- 19,895 security personnel mobilised; 8,186 people rescued including 279 evacuated from a hydropower tunnel by helicopter over two days and 227 foreign nationals.
- Relief: Rasuwa and Nuwakot each received Rs 100 million, Dhading Rs 50 million; Rs 675 million distributed across 15 local units; fuel reserves positioned in Nuwakot for rescue machinery.

## Relevance

The district-level body-recovery geography is independent physical evidence of the flood route downstream of the hydropower corridor — it anchors the Narayani reaches of the [[bhotekoshi-trishuli-flood-2026]] route layer and quantifies the corridor's human toll alongside the project-level damage in [[kathmandupost-bhotekoshi-flood-hydropower-damage-2026]].

## Limitations

- Figures are a single 9 a.m. update on 30 August 2026; tolls and missing counts were still moving.
- Body-recovery locations indicate where victims were found, which approximates but does not exactly equal flood extent; hydraulic transport downstream complicates inference.
- The 933 hydropower-linked missing count is a government aggregate; IPPAN the previous day reported 934 across 11 projects — the two counts have not been reconciled publicly.
