# UAHEL Upper Arun Hydroelectric Project Page

## Summary

Official UAHEL project page for the Upper Arun Hydroelectric Project, providing current public project parameters, power-evacuation plan, financing-preparation status, environmental/social status, and target construction schedule.

## Key Findings

- UAHEL lists Upper Arun as a **1,063.36 MW** peaking run-of-river project, including a **2.36 MW** eco-flow plant.
- The current project page reports rated discharge of **235.44 m3/s**, rated head of **508.26 m**, and average annual energy of **4,518 GWh** excluding the eco-flow turbine.
- The project page reports year-round minimum peaking of **697 MW** for **six hours** daily at Q95 inflow.
- Power evacuation is planned through a **5.8 km 400 kV double-circuit** line to Haitar substation in Sankhuwasabha.
- UAHEL reports target entry into construction in **2026** and target completion in **December 2032**.

## Limitations

- **Developer source.** This is the project company's own public page and should be cross-checked against NEA annual reports, DoED licenses, lender documents, and environmental approvals.
- **Preparation-stage status.** The page describes project preparation and target schedule; it does not prove financial closure, PPA execution, or construction commencement.
- **Dynamic page.** The project page may update without a versioned archive, so numbers should be rechecked during any future deep-research pass.
