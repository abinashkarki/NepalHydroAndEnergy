# Kathmandu Post — India renews export approvals for four Nepali hydropower projects (2026)

## Summary

This 6 October 2026 report covers India's renewal of export approvals for four Nepali projects whose earlier approvals had lapsed between June and August 2026. It also describes how the August 2026 Bhotekoshi flood affected export-approved plants.

## Key Claims

- The renewed projects: Upper Chameliya 38.8 MW (approval expired in June), Seti river 24.25 MW (expired 31 July), Upper Tadi Khola 10.7 MW (expired 31 July) and Upper Dordi A 24.25 MW (expired in August).
- India has authorised export from 37 hydropower projects, with a combined capacity of about 1,200 MW.
- Trishuli, Chilime and Devighat, all export-approved, were damaged by the flood, and exports to Bangladesh stopped. Kabeli B-1 and Solu Khola are approved for IEX sale. NEA has asked for Lower Modi to be approved for the bilateral market in place of Devighat.
