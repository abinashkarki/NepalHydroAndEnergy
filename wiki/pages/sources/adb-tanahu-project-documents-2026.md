# ADB Tanahu Hydropower Project Documents (2026)

## Summary

ADB's active project page and document register for the 140 MW Tanahu
Hydropower Project. The page provides financing, contracts, audited
statements and current environmental and social monitoring documents.

## Key Findings

- Project status: active; installed capacity: 140 MW storage hydro.
- ADB disclosed FY 2024/25 audited project financial statements in
  January 2026.
- A ninth environmental and social Panel of Experts report was
  disclosed in April 2026.
- The page provides loan utilisation, contract awards, covenant status
  and tender records.
- The 2013 appraisal estimated project completion by 31 December 2020.
- For Loan 2990, the original closing date of 30 June 2021 is now
  revised to 30 December 2028.
- The contract register records the original headworks award in 2018,
  two headworks rebidding notices in 2019 and 2020, and the replacement
  `HEADWORKS (REBID-II)` award in April 2021.

## Relevance

This is the primary monitoring feed for [[tanahu-hydropower]]. It
supports package, finance and safeguard tracking rather than reliance
on a single overall progress percentage.

## Limitations

The project page's last project-data-sheet update, live financial data
and individual document dates differ. The appraisal completion estimate,
loan closing date, contract award and commissioning are separate
milestones and should not be treated as interchangeable. An overall
public progress percentage from another source should not be attributed
to ADB unless it appears in an ADB document.
