# NEA Annual Report FY 2025/26

## Summary

*A Year in Review, Fiscal Year 2025/2026* is the Nepal Electricity Authority's annual report for the year ending mid-July 2026 (Ashadh 2083). NEA published it on 18 August 2026. It supersedes [[nea-annual-report-fy2024-25]] as the latest official system-wide snapshot. Page numbers below are PDF pages, with printed page numbers in brackets.

## Project and Grid Status Stated in the Report

- **Tanahu (140 MW):** about 78% complete across three construction packages (PDF pp.13, 16).
- **Hetauda–Dhalkebar 400 kV:** the decade-long construction dispute "has finally been resolved", with completion expected "in few months" (PDF pp.13–14). See [[hetauda-dhalkebar-inaruwa-backbone]] for later status.
- **Commissioned in FY 2025/26:** Madhya Bhotekoshi (102 MW); Khimti–Barhabise 400 kV; Markichowk–Bharatpur 220 kV; Kohalpur–Surkhet 132 kV (PDF p.13).
- **Dudhkoshi Storage (670 MW):** detailed design and safeguard studies advancing with ADB support; land acquisition and access-road works under way (PDF p.15).
- **Cross-border lines:** Dhalkebar–Sitamarhi 400 kV and Butwal–Gorakhpur 400 kV nearing completion. The July 2026 Joint Steering Committee approved interim 220 kV operation of Butwal–Gorakhpur and a combined Dhalkebar–Muzaffarpur/Sitamarhi limit of 1,650 MW export and 1,400 MW import (PDF p.14).
- **Curtailment:** NEA names minimising generation curtailment as a challenge but publishes no curtailment volume (PDF p.12).

## Relevance to Project

This is the primary source for the current system baseline in [[state-of-the-system]] and the seasonal pattern in [[seasonal-mismatch]]. FY 2025/26 matters because Nepal was a large net exporter for the year, yet still imported 273 GWh in Falgun while exporting 13 GWh. Annual surplus did not remove the dry-season deficit.

## Limitations

- The summary and the energy-balance table use different trade totals; do not mix them in one calculation.
- "Energy available" (18,624 GWh) and "monthly system energy demand" (19,078 GWh) are different aggregates.
- Installed capacity is a nameplate figure; it says nothing about dependable dry-season output.
- Profit and loss figures are pre-audit statements in the managing director's report.

## What This Source Does Not Cover

- Plant-level dispatch, availability declarations or curtailment volumes.
- Events after mid-July 2026, including the August 2026 [[bhotekoshi-trishuli-flood-2026]].
- Hydraulic type (RoR, PRoR, storage) for every private plant.
