# NEFPORT Protected-Area Hydropower Ruling Estimate (2025)

## Summary

Sector-facing estimate of hydropower exposure after Nepal's early-2025
Supreme Court ruling on development activities in protected areas.

## Key Data Points

- NEFPORT Issue 60 reports the sector estimate that the ruling affects
  **267 hydropower projects** totaling **19,736 MW**.
- The estimate is sourced to industry / IPPAN-linked reporting rather
  than to a quantified Supreme Court project table.

## Relevance

Use for claims about the possible hydropower-project exposure of the
protected-area ruling. Wording should say "industry groups estimate" or
"sector estimates," not "the court found."

## Limitations

- The 267 / 19,736 MW figure is an industry exposure estimate.
- It is not a neutral court quantification and should not be treated as
  a legal annex to the ruling.
