# Budhigandaki Jalbidhyut Company Project Record and Notices (2026)

## Summary

The official BGJCL project website and notice register, checked in July
2026. The site provides current headline technical parameters, company
identity, procurement notices and an annual-report section.

## Key Findings

- Published capacity: 1,200 MW.
- Published annual energy: 3,338 GWh.
- Published dam height: 263 m.
- The company describes the project as a storage project on the Budhi
  Gandaki River.
- Notices visible in June and July 2026 included financial-bid opening,
  an addendum and a letter of intent dated 8 July 2026.
- The annual-report section lists an annual report for FY 2079/80.

## Relevance

This source provides the current company baseline for [[budhigandaki]]
and supports treating procurement notices as dated events rather than
as construction or financing completion.

## Limitations

The website does not provide a consolidated current dashboard for
financing close, resettlement completion, physical construction or an
approved commercial-operation schedule. A notice or letter of intent
does not establish construction commencement.
