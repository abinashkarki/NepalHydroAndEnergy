# Embassy of India Release on the Motihari–Amlekhgunj Pipeline (2019)

## Summary

This is the Embassy of India's press release of 10 September 2019 on the joint inauguration of the cross-border petroleum products pipeline from Motihari (India) to Amlekhgunj (Nepal).

## Key Findings

- The release describes "the 69 km pipeline, which starts from Motihari in India and ends at Amlekhgunj in Nepal".
- It states: "The 10” diameter pipeline has a capacity of 2 Million Metric Ton Per Annum (MMTPA)."
- Indian Oil Corporation built the pipeline, investing over INR 324 crore, in collaboration with Nepal Oil Corporation.
- Both companies were working on additional storage at Amlekhgunj Depot, with an NOC commitment of about INR 75 crore.

## Limitations

- This is the inauguration-day release. It does not report later throughput, utilisation or completed storage additions.
- It does not split the 69 km between India and Nepal, and it does not name the specific products carried.
