# VUCL Mugu Karnali Storage HEP Project Page

## Summary

VUCL's official project page for the proposed 1,902 MW Mugu Karnali
Storage HEP. It publishes the current salient design parameters and
identifies NEA Engineering Company as design consultant.

## Key Findings

- Project type: storage; installed capacity: 1,902 MW.
- Catchment area: 16,098.3 km²; design discharge: 793.45 m³/s.
- Dam height: 283 m.
- Reservoir storage: 4,843.2 MCM total and 2,238.95 MCM live.
- Generation configuration: eight Francis units at 242.67 MW each.
- Annual design energy: 6,291.8 GWh, comprising 2,415 GWh dry-season
  and 3,876.8 GWh wet-season energy.
- Evacuation concept: approximately 40 km of 400 kV double-circuit line.
- Published implementation duration: ten years; debt/equity assumption:
  75:25.

## Relevance

This is the primary design source for [[mugu-karnali-storage]]. It
corrects earlier wiki values of ten units, 5,883 GWh and an unsupported
pumped-storage classification.

## Limitations

The parameters remain feasibility-stage values, not final construction
quantities. The page's cost entry is written as “46,000 Crore” without
enough currency and cost-basis context for safe conversion. It does not
establish financing close, selected PPP ownership or construction start.
