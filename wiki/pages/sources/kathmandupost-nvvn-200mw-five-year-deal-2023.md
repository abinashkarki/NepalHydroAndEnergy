# Kathmandu Post — Nepal to sell 200MW under 5-year deal (2023)

## Summary

This 26 June 2023 report describes NEA's agreement to sell 200 MW to India's NTPC Vidyut Vyapar Nigam (NVVN) for five years, in the wet season only. It names the five projects supplying the power.

## Key Claims

- The projects and their export quantum: Solukhola (Dudhkoshi) 83.42 MW, Super Madi 42.68 MW, Upper Balephi 'A' 34.92 MW, Dordi Khola 26.19 MW and Upper Chaku 'A' 12.79 MW. Together they make 200 MW.
- The net tariff is INR 5.25 (NRs 8.40) per unit, after trade margin, transmission losses and transmission charges.
