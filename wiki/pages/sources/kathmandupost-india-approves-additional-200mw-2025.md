# Kathmandu Post — India approves purchase of additional 200 MW from Nepal (2025)

## Summary

This 12 August 2025 report covers India's approval of a further 199.70 MW from six Nepali projects, which raised the approved total to 1,140.70 MW from 34 projects. It names the 14 projects selling in the IEX day-ahead and real-time markets and the two projects supplying Bangladesh.

## Key Claims

- The approved total was 1,140.70 MW from 34 projects, up from 941 MW from 28 projects a year earlier and 690 MW from 16 projects before that.
- The 14 IEX projects and their capacities: Trishuli 23.28, Devighat 14.55, Marsyangdi 67, Kali Gandaki A 140, Middle Marsyangdi 68, Likhu IV 51, Chilime 21.44, Solu Khola 22.80, Kabeli B1 24.25, Lower Modi 19.40, Upper Kaligandaki 32.70, Upper Chamelia 37.30, Upper Dordi A 24.25 and Upper Balephi A 34.92 MW.
- Bangladesh buys 40 MW: Trishuli 18.60 MW and Chilime 21.40 MW, from 15 June to 15 November each year, at 6.40 US cents per unit. The tripartite agreement was signed on 3 October 2024.
- The sale to Haryana rose to 435.2 MW from 235.5 MW. Bihar buys 125.004 MW from 10 projects over the Kataiya, Raxaul and Ramnagar 132 kV lines.

## Limitations

- It does not give the approved quantum for each IEX project, and it does not name the six newly approved projects or the Haryana and Bihar supplier plants.
- Its count of 14 IEX projects differs from the 13 IEX approvals in NEA's FY 2025/26 table (11 via Dhalkebar-Muzaffarpur and 2 via Tanakpur-Mahendranagar).
