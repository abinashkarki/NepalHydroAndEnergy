# IEA / Tracking SDG7 Energy Progress Report 2025

## Summary

Current global electricity-access benchmark used to replace older
`675 million` access claims in documentary and wiki-facing material.

## Key Data Points

- The 2025 Tracking SDG7 release reports that **over 666 million people**
  lacked access to electricity in 2023.
- The same release frames global basic electricity access at roughly
  **92%** of the world population.

## Relevance

Use this source when citing the current global electricity-access gap.
Older `675 million` wording reflects an earlier Tracking SDG7 estimate
and should not be used for current narration without a year label.

## Limitations

- This is a global access benchmark, not Nepal-specific.
- The headline figure refers to **2023** access status, published in
  the 2025 report cycle.
