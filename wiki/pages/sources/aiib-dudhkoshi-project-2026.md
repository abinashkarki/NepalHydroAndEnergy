# AIIB Dudhkoshi Storage Hydroelectric Project Summary (2026)

## Summary

AIIB's proposed-project summary for the Dudhkoshi Storage Hydroelectric
Project, published in February 2026. It records the current proposed
financing envelope, technical configuration, implementing entities and
preliminary environmental and social risk profile.

## Key Findings

- Project status: proposed; AIIB environmental and social Category A.
- Proposed AIIB sovereign financing: USD 200 million, with ADB named as
  lead co-financier.
- Current configuration: 220 m dam, 600 MW underground main powerhouse
  and 70 MW dam-toe ecological-flow powerhouse.
- Preliminary footprint: approximately 2,405 hectares of permanent land
  acquisition and about 3,300 affected households, including an
  estimated 290 physically and economically displaced households.
- The source identifies land acquisition, Indigenous Peoples,
  biodiversity, sediment, slope stability, seismic, dam-safety and GLOF
  risks and lists assessment and management instruments still under
  preparation or review.

## Relevance

This is the current primary status and safeguard source for
[[dudhkoshi-storage]]. It distinguishes proposed financing from
approval, effectiveness, disbursement and financial close.

## Limitations

The document is a proposed-project summary, not an approval document,
financing agreement, final design report or construction notice. Its
social-impact figures are preliminary, and transmission-line and
access-road impacts were still being assessed.
