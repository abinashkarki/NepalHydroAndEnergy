# Timilsina et al. CGE Load Shedding Cost Study (2021)

## Summary

Peer-reviewed computable general equilibrium (CGE) study quantifying the macroeconomic cost of Nepal's electricity load shedding crisis (2008–2016). Published in *Renewable and Sustainable Energy Reviews*. This is the canonical source for the GDP-loss estimate that anchors Beat 1.1 of the production bible.

## Key Data Points

- **Total GDP lost 2008–2016:** ~US$11 billion (approximately Nepal's entire 2008 GDP)
- **Annual GDP loss at peak:** ~7% of GDP per year
- **Annual cost:** up to US$1.6 billion/year at worst
- **Investment suppression:** annual investment approximately 48% below potential during the shedding era
- **Methodology:** CGE model calibrated to Nepal's input-output structure, capturing direct production losses and indirect supply-chain effects
- **Peak daily shedding:** 14–18 hours/day during the worst dry seasons (2009–2016)

## Relevance to Project

Central source for [[load-shedding-era]] and the historical context that frames the entire wiki narrative. The CGE methodology captures not just direct outage costs (lost industrial output) but indirect losses (investment deterrence, firm entry suppression, substitution to costly diesel backup). This makes the $11 billion figure more comprehensive than simple output-loss estimates.
