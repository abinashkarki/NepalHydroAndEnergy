# Kantipur — Approval to sell an additional 80 MW of electricity in India (2025)

## Summary

This Bhadra 6, 2082 (22 August 2025) report covers Indian approval for two more Nepali hydropower projects to sell electricity in India.

## Key Claims

- The projects approved were Mewa Khola (48.5 MW) and Karuwa Seti (31.04 MW). The headline rounds their 79.54 MW total to 80 MW.

## Relevance

This report is the source for the Mewa Khola and Karuwa Seti `plant-approval` rows in [[data-cross-border-approved-plants]].

## Limitations

- The report gives one MW figure per project and does not separate installed capacity from approved quantum. It does not name the market channel or the validity period.
