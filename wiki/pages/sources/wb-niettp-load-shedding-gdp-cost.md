# World Bank NIETTP Load-Shedding GDP-Cost Estimate

## Summary

World Bank project document for the Nepal-India Electricity Transmission
and Trade Project that contains the specific load-shedding cost estimate
used in the documentary verification pass.

## Key Data Points

- The document describes Nepal's 2007-2017 electricity shortage period,
  including load shedding of up to roughly 14 hours per day.
- It states that load shedding cost Nepal an estimated **7% of GDP
  annually** during that era.

## Relevance

This is the correct World Bank citation for the `7% of GDP` load-shedding
cost claim. The World Bank Country Economic Memorandum 2025 supports the
post-load-shedding reliability context but does not contain this exact
GDP-cost estimate on the checked page.

## Limitations

- The estimate describes the load-shedding era, not current outage costs.
- It should be cited as a World Bank project-document estimate, not as a
  new 2025 CEM finding.
