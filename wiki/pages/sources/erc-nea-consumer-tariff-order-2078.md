# ERC — NEA Electricity Consumer Tariff Determination 2078

## Summary

This is the Electricity Regulatory Commission's full decision on Nepal Electricity Authority's consumer tariff (petition CT-2/2078/079), including community wholesale consumers. The cover page says the Commission set the rates at its 140th meeting on 2078/07/08 BS (25 October 2021). The rates apply to electricity consumed from Mangsir 2078 and to bills issued from Paush 2078. The 112-page PDF is listed on the ERC "charge order" page (erc.gov.np/charge-order), uploaded 2082-11-03 BS.

## Key Claims

- The tariff schedule is printed on PDF pp.74–83. It covers domestic single-phase slabs by ampere rating, three-phase domestic, other consumers at low and higher voltage, time-of-day rates for Baishakh–Mangsir and Paush–Chaitra, swap-card transport rates without a demand charge, and community wholesale rates.
- Domestic 5 A consumers pay NRs 0/unit for 0–20 units against a NRs 30 minimum charge. Above 20 units, the first 20 are charged at NRs 3.00/unit (p.74 note).
- Charging-station operators may add at most 20% per unit when serving electric vehicles (p.83 note).
- Service charges and fees start on p.84.

## Relevance

This is the regulatory order behind the retail schedule that NEA reprints in its annual-report tariff appendix. Every non-zero charge in [[data-erc-retail-tariffs]] was matched against the order's tariff pages, and the `effective_from` date in that CSV comes from this order.

## Limitations

- The order is in Nepali. The effective date is stated as a billing month (Mangsir 2078 consumption), not as a calendar day. Mangsir 1, 2078 BS is 17 November 2021 by standard calendar conversion.
- As of 2026-11-25, the charge-order page lists no later NEA consumer tariff order. Whether VAT or later fees apply on top of these rates is outside this document.
