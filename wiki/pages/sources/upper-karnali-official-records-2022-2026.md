# Upper Karnali Official Records — IBN and DoED (2022–2026)

## Summary

This source record combines two primary Government of Nepal records relevant to the Upper Karnali Hydroelectric Project: Investment Board Nepal's 2021/22 annual report and the Department of Electricity Development's registry updated 2 July 2026. Together they document a conditional financial-close extension and the project's current administrative registry listing.

## Key Findings

- Investment Board Nepal records that the Government of Nepal approved a two-year extension of the financial-close deadline in July 2022, subject to conditions.
- The IBN record describes the developer as being in a process to meet the negotiation conditions; it does not report financial close as achieved.
- The DoED registry updated 2 July 2026 lists Upper Karnali HEP at 900 MW on the Karnali River, with GMR Upper Karnali Hydropower Limited as promoter.
- The DoED page places the project in its more-than-1-MW survey-licence table. That administrative listing establishes a current registry record, not construction, financing or PPA status.

## Relevance

These official records provide a primary institutional baseline for [[upper-karnali]] and distinguish a deadline extension or registry listing from achieved financial close, lender disbursement, executed offtake or construction commencement.

## Limitations

- Neither record contains an executed financing agreement, lender effectiveness notice, construction notice to proceed, signed buyer PPA or tripartite transit approval.
- The IBN annual-report entry is historical and cannot establish the position after its reporting period.
- The DoED registry's licence category is administrative; it should not be inferred to be a physical delivery-stage classification.
