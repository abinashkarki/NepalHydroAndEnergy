# NEA Operational-Reporting Portals (2026)

## Summary

NEA's public Generation Directorate operational-report page and related
Generation Directorate and Transmission Directorate publication portals,
reviewed on 10 July and rechecked on 13 July 2026. The pages establish the visible public
publication boundary for generation and system-operation reporting.

## Key Findings

- The Generation Directorate's `Generation Operational Reports` page
  displayed `Coming soon` on the review date.
- The Generation Directorate homepage labels a `Monthly Generation
  Reports` area but displayed no records on the review date.
- A 13 July 2026 recheck found the same publication boundary: the
  operational-report page still displayed `Coming soon`, while the
  homepage's monthly-generation area still displayed `No Record Found`.
- NEA's public daily Transmission Directorate reports provide aggregate
  daily system generation, trade, demand and peak fields; their published
  form does not supply plant-level availability, curtailment-event reason
  codes or dispatch-order logs.

## Relevance

This source records why [[stranded-generation]] remains a data-acquisition
question. It supports the distinction between publicly available aggregate
system observations and the separate plant-level dispatch records needed
to quantify curtailment.

## Limitations

- Portal visibility does not establish whether NEA holds internal source
  records, whether a report is published elsewhere, or whether a requested
  record can be disclosed.
- The review is a dated snapshot and may change if NEA publishes the
  operational-report section later.
- The daily-report observation is about the published aggregate fields;
  it is not a claim that those reports are a complete system-operation
  archive.
