# Electricity Regulatory Commission (ERC)

## Summary

The **Electricity Regulatory Commission (विद्युत नियमन आयोग)** is Nepal's electricity-sector regulator. Its website says it was established on 2076 Baisakh 23 BS under the Electricity Regulatory Commission Act 2074 and the ERC Regulations 2075, and describes it as an autonomous and independent regulatory body.

## Role in Nepal's Energy System

ERC's stated functions are to regulate electricity tariffs, protect consumer rights and interests, and make generation, transmission, distribution and trade orderly and transparent. Its site publishes PPA-consent application lists, so it is also part of the approval path for power purchase agreements. NEA's FY 2025/26 annual report records that NEA's Regulatory Compliance Department prepared an electricity tariff petition under ERC regulations and submitted a KPI report to ERC (PDF p.153).

On the purchase side, ERC issued the [[erc-reservoir-ppa-directive-2082|reservoir PPA directive 2082]] and commissioned the [[erc-eca-storage-ppa-discussion-paper-2025|2025 storage PPA discussion paper]]. Public reporting also records an [[erc-open-access-directive-2082|open access directive 2082]].

## Key Datasets & Disclosures

- Consumer tariff-fixation directive (2083) and tariff orders
- Directives on open access, captive plants and metering (2083 series)
- Annual reports
- Application-status lists for PPA consent, IPO and rights-share pre-approval, and share-structure changes
- Commission meeting decisions and hearing orders

The retail tariff values held in this wiki ([[data-erc-retail-tariffs]]) are transcribed from NEA's annual report, not from an ERC order.

## Limitations & Controversies

The website does not state the Commission's composition or its budget. This page does not assess the Commission's independence in practice or how consistently its decisions are enforced.
