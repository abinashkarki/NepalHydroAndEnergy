# Hydroelectricity Investment and Development Company Limited (HIDCL)

## Summary

**Hydroelectricity Investment and Development Company Limited (HIDCL)** is a state-promoted company that invests in hydropower and other energy projects. Its website says it was formally established on 11 July 2011 under a Government of Nepal decision. Its stated mission is to mobilise funds from domestic and international sources.

## Role in Nepal's Energy System

HIDCL provides equity and loan finance to energy projects. Its website names Seti, Mistri Khola, Solu, Solukhola and Lower Likhu among the projects it has invested in, but gives no MW figures. Its portfolio statistics counter showed zero on the review date.

NEA's FY 2025/26 annual report records HIDCL in two financing roles:

- leading coordination with financial institutions such as the Social Security Fund and Citizen Investment Trust towards financial closure of a project described on PDF p.115
- joining a lending consortium with NMB Bank and Sanima Bank for Modi Jalvidyut Company's projects (PDF p.116)

HIDCL is also listed as a promoter shareholder of Vidhyut Utpadan Company Limited (VUCL).
