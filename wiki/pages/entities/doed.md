# Department of Electricity Development (DoED)

## Summary

The **Department of Electricity Development (विद्युत विकास विभाग)** is the government department under [[moewri]] that licenses electricity projects. DoED's introduction page says it began as the Electricity Development Center on 16 July 1993 (2050 Shrawan 1) and was renamed DoED on 7 February 2000 (2056 Magh 24).

## Role in Nepal's Energy System

DoED's site describes it as supporting the ministry in implementing power-sector policy and encouraging private investment through a "One Window" service and project licensing. Its divisions cover project study, licensing, inspection and monitoring, planning, finance and legal work. It also hosts government-led storage and multipurpose projects, including Saptakoshi High Dam, Pancheshwar, Budhigandaki and Naumure, as well as a royalty-management system.

Licensing rules such as the Q45 design-discharge requirement come from DoED's [[doed-licensing-directive-2075|licensing directive 2075]].

## Key Datasets & Disclosures

- Survey and generation licence lists for hydro (below and above 1 MW), solar, wind, thermal, biomass, cogeneration and transmission
- Survey and generation application lists
- Operating power-plant lists by technology; the solar list is recorded in [[doed-solar-power-plants-table]]
- Cancelled-licence lists, Investment Board project lists and the government project bank
- A licence-performance dashboard and an online licence-application system

The wiki's processed DoED hydropower registry (`data/processed/tables/doed_hydropower_registry.csv`) is built from these lists. It also feeds the promoter ranking in [[data-major-developers]].

## Limitations & Controversies

The lists are administrative records. A licence does not show that construction or financing has happened, and the lists' update frequency is not stated on the site.
