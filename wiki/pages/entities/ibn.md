# Investment Board Nepal (IBN)

## Summary

The **Investment Board (लगानी बोर्ड)**, administered by the Office of the Investment Board in Kathmandu, is a high-level government body chaired by the Prime Minister. Its website describes its purpose as creating an investment-friendly environment and mobilising and managing domestic and foreign investment.

## Role in Nepal's Energy System

IBN handles investment facilitation for large projects. Its pages do not list an energy-project portfolio. The overlap with licensing is visible on the [[doed]] website, which publishes separate Investment Board project lists: proposal stage, survey licences, generation-licence applications and generation licences. Those lists are the most direct public record of which electricity projects fall under the Investment Board track. They are not yet extracted in this wiki.
