# Nepal Digital Infrastructure Company Limited (NDICL)

## Summary

**Nepal Digital Infrastructure Company Limited (NDICL)** is a 100% subsidiary of the [[nea|Nepal Electricity Authority]], established on 9 March 2026. NEA says it was founded to support the Digital Nepal Framework by using NEA's physical assets (poles, transmission lines and rights-of-way) to build a national digital backbone. It works on two pillars: optical fibre infrastructure and data-centre services ([[nea-annual-report-fy2025-26]], PDF p.138, printed p.127).

## Role in Nepal's Energy System

NDICL puts NEA's grid corridors to a second use. NEA presents the model as a new revenue stream for NEA, lower build-out costs for telecom operators that share utility infrastructure, and a secure framework for government data exchange. These are NEA's stated aims, not measured outcomes.

For siting energy-intensive digital loads, such as data centres, NDICL is the institution most likely to hold fibre routes along the transmission network. See [[data-clean-industry-and-compute-siting]] for the grid and tariff facts that are public today.
