# Ministry of Energy, Water Resources and Irrigation (MoEWRI)

## Summary

The **Ministry of Energy, Water Resources and Irrigation (ऊर्जा, जलस्रोत तथा सिँचाइ मन्त्रालय)** is the federal ministry responsible for electricity, water resources and irrigation policy. Its introduction page says it was formed by merging the former Ministry of Energy and the former Ministry of Irrigation. The Alternative Energy Promotion Centre and the Department of Hydrology and Meteorology were moved into it from the former environment ministry.

## Role in Nepal's Energy System

According to the ministry's introduction page, its functions include:

- policy, law, standards and regulation for water and energy
- national and international transmission lines and electricity trade agreements
- tariff and service-quality policy
- the national and regional load-dispatch system
- renewable and alternative energy standards
- water and energy data and research
- oversight of linked public bodies and companies

Its website lists [[wecs]], [[doed]], [[nea]], [[erc]], [[aepc]], VUCL, [[rpgcl]], [[hidcl]], DWRI and NNRFC as related bodies.

The ministry also issued the 2025 integrated power-system plan summarised in [[moewri-ipsdp-exec-summary-2025]].
