# Institutions, Finance and Project Delivery

## The delivery chain

Nepal's electricity system is shaped by ministries, the Department of Electricity Development, Nepal Electricity Authority, the Electricity Regulatory Commission, project companies, lenders, investors, local governments and affected communities. A project must pass through several different evidence stages: concept, licence, studies, safeguards, offtake, financing, construction, grid connection, commissioning and operation.

This wiki does not treat one stage as proof of the next. A licence is not financial close; a letter of intent is not construction; a reported progress percentage is not synchronised commissioning; installed capacity is not reliable delivery.

## Finance and risk allocation

Hydropower and transmission require large upfront capital and long construction periods. PPA terms, exchange-rate exposure, hydrology, geology, cost escalation, equity disclosure and completion guarantees all affect who bears risk. Public-company and lender records can illuminate individual projects, but they do not yet form a consistent sector-wide performance dataset.

## Regulatory and operating roles

NEA remains central across procurement, transmission, system operation and distribution. The ERC has a distinct regulatory mandate. Debate over structural reform should distinguish what the law establishes, what directives actually require and what remains a policy option. Those choices are presented as [[intervention-nea-structural-separation|Decision Dossiers]], not settled fact.

## Delivery evidence

The flagship project records—[[upper-tamakoshi]], [[arun-3]], [[dudhkoshi-storage]], [[upper-karnali]] and [[budhigandaki]]—show why status provenance matters. Each should state the date, source and scope behind its status and preserve unknowns rather than blend registry, developer and media claims.

## Evidence gaps

- Comparable project-level cost and schedule baselines.
- Executed financing and offtake documents for proposed projects.
- A complete public PPA and contingent-liability inventory.
- Machine-readable issuer disclosures and use-of-proceeds tracking.
- Dated coordination evidence between generation and evacuation assets.
