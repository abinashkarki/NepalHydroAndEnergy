# Twenty-Year Strategy

A rational 20-year water-and-energy strategy for Nepal, synthesized from the strategy and evidence pages.

## In plain language

Nepal's electricity system has a simple-sounding problem with a complicated solution: **the rivers are full in summer and nearly empty in winter**. Most power plants are "run-of-river," meaning they generate when the river flows and under-produce when it does not. The fix is not "build more of the same." It is **build the right mix, in the right order**.

The 2035 adequacy model has changed what "the right mix" means. The old strategy was "build storage hydro and wait." The new strategy is a **four-lever portfolio**: solar for winter daytime energy, storage hydro + BESS for evening capacity, demand-shaping to shift load, and — for the post-2035 evening peak — Budhigandaki-scale firm capacity if Nepal chooses to build it.

This page sequences those levers across three phases.

## Phase 1: 2024–2030 — Complete and Connect

- Complete all projects under construction (including [[tanahu-hydropower]])
- **Scale solar aggressively:** deliver the 960 MW tender, build the next 500–1,000 MW, and integrate BESS into future tenders
- **Fix land and interconnection:** pass agrivoltaic lease law, upgrade substations, and build solar-ready grid headroom
- Build Butwal-Gorakhpur cross-border line
- Begin east-west 400 kV transmission backbone
- Negotiate Bangladesh electricity transit arrangement through India
- Pilot first pumped storage or grid-scale BESS (200–500 MW)
- Use cheap power to begin attracting energy-intensive industry

**Key interventions:** [[intervention-transmission-completion]] (Hetauda–Dhalkebar, Butwal–Gorakhpur); [[intervention-nea-structural-separation]] (begin ISO and open-access work in this phase).

**Priority:** Fix [[stranded-generation]] by building grid before licensing more generation. The 2035 model shows solar is the fastest winter-energy lever, but only if the grid can absorb it.

## Phase 2: 2030–2037 — Store and Industrialize

- Develop [[karnali-basin]] corridor (first 2,000 MW)
- Complete east-west transmission backbone
- Add two more India cross-border lines
- Bring [[dudhkoshi-storage]] online (~670 MW storage + 200 MW pumped storage)
- Build [[budhigandaki]] (1,200 MW storage) — the firm-capacity option for the evening peak
- Activate Bangladesh export contracts at scale
- Use cheap reliable power to attract ferrosilicon, cement, and [[green-hydrogen-nepal]] investment

**Key interventions:** [[intervention-electric-cooking-transition]] (build domestic demand to absorb monsoon surplus and shift evening peak); [[intervention-q-design-climate-adjustment]] (mandate climate-adjusted hydrology before licensing the Phase 2 pipeline); [[intervention-sebon-data-transparency]] (ensure Phase 2 IPOs are transparently disclosed).

**Priority:** Add [[storage-deficit]] relief and close the evening firm-capacity gap. The 2035 model shows that by 2035, solar + Tanahu + Dudhkoshi + BESS + demand-shaping closes the total winter energy gap, but a ~933 MW evening shortage remains. Budhigandaki is the decisive asset for that evening peak.

## Phase 3: 2037–2044 — Leverage and Transform

- 10,000+ MW installed
- Major storage operational
- Industrial electricity demand absorbing 30–40% of generation
- Export revenue $500M+/year
- Renegotiate Koshi and Gandak treaties from position of infrastructure strength
- Design for [[peak-water]] post-peak decline

**Key interventions:** All preceding interventions should be institutionalised by this phase. The domestic demand built through [[intervention-electric-cooking-transition]] should be absorbing 30–40% of generation. [[intervention-nea-structural-separation]] should be fully legislated, with an independent ISO operating the grid. [[intervention-q-design-climate-adjustment]] should be the licensing baseline for all projects, not just MDB-financed ones.

**Priority:** Use energy as foundation for [[domestic-led-hydro-strategy]] economic transformation.

## Six Principles

1. **Move from capacity maximization to [[firm-power]] maximization** — megawatts do not matter if they arrive at the wrong time
2. **Build a portfolio, not a single lever** — solar + storage hydro + BESS + demand-shaping, sequenced so slippage in one does not collapse the whole
3. **Treat transmission as part of generation economics** — every basin plan judged with evacuation; 5 GW solar needs grid headroom
4. **Build domestic demand at the same time** — don't wait for industry to appear; demand-shaping is virtual capacity
5. **Upgrade for climate resilience** — better hydromet, [[sediment-as-design-constraint]] monitoring, [[glof-risk]] design standards
6. **Think in basins and portfolios, not just projects** — project-by-project opportunism is the wrong scale

## Precondition

All of this requires **political stability** enabling multi-decade commitment and **[[nea]] capability building** that matches ambition. Without institutional reform — specifically [[intervention-nea-structural-separation]] and [[intervention-transmission-completion]] in Phase 1 — the engineering plans are fiction.
