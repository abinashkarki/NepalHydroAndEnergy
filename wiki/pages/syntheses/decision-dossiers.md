# Decision Dossiers and Policy Options

Decision Dossiers retain the repository's `interventions` category for technical compatibility while changing the public editorial contract. Each asks a bounded decision question and distinguishes evidence from assessment.

## Dossiers

- [[intervention-transmission-completion]] — transmission delivery and settlement
- [[intervention-nea-structural-separation]] — system operation, regulatory roles and open access
- [[intervention-sebon-data-transparency]] — hydropower financial disclosure
- [[intervention-electric-cooking-transition]] — cooking electrification and distribution readiness
- [[intervention-q-design-climate-adjustment]] — climate-adjusted hydrology in project design

## Required sections

Every dossier contains: **Decision Question**, **Established Facts**, **Unknowns**, **Comparable Cases**, **Response Options and Trade-offs**, and **TransparentGov Assessment**. Comparable cases must say why transfer may fail; absence of a defensible case is recorded as an evidence gap rather than filled with analogy.
