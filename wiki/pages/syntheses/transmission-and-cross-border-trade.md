# Transmission and Cross-Border Electricity Trade

## Why this branch matters

Generation has value only when it can reach a load or an authorised market. Nepal's internal grid, substations, cross-border lines, export approvals and dispatch rules therefore form one delivery chain. A constraint at any point can strand otherwise available generation.

## What is established

- Nepal reported annual electricity exports above imports in FY2024/25, while dry-season imports continued.
- The Hetauda–Dhalkebar–Inaruwa 400 kV backbone remains a high-value internal corridor. NEA maintenance notices show conductor-stringing work on the Hetauda–Dhalkebar section on 3 and 5 July 2026.
- Those notices do **not** establish a final tower count, commercial energisation or corridor completion.
- The public evidence supports transmission and market access as material constraints. It does not provide the plant-level dispatch data required to calculate a current national curtailment total or rank transmission above every other constraint.

## Cross-border trade

India is Nepal's principal electricity-market interface. Bangladesh trade uses Indian transmission territory and therefore adds a buyer without removing corridor and regulatory dependence. Trade volumes, prices and approval scope must be dated because they change faster than the physical river system.

## Community and delivery process

The Bharatpur–Bardaghat case shows that a mediated process can resolve a specific transmission dispute: NEA and 63 community members reached an agreement concerning the final two towers after a World Bank Dispute Resolution Service process. Its terms were confidential, and its voluntary, case-specific structure does not prove that the model transfers unchanged to other corridors. See the evidence-led [[intervention-transmission-completion|transmission Decision Dossier]].

## Data gaps

- Circuit- and substation-level available transfer capability.
- Dated synchronisation of generation and evacuation assets.
- Plant-level dispatch, congestion and curtailment reason codes.
- A single reconciled public table of export approvals, contract terms and actual delivery.
