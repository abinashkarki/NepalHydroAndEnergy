# Plant Annual Generation FY 2024/25–2025/26

## Summary

This page records annual generation for each NEA-owned plant and for Upper Tamakoshi, an NEA subsidiary, for FY 2024/25 and FY 2025/26. NEA-owned plant figures come from the Generation Directorate's monthly generation tables. Upper Tamakoshi figures come from the Business Development Directorate narrative. The NEA annual reports do not publish generation for individual IPP plants or for the other subsidiary plants, so those volumes appear only as residuals in the reconciliation below.

For NEA-owned plants, the plant rows sum exactly to the Generation Directorate's "Grand Total": 2,902.40 GWh in FY 2024/25 and 2,763.73 GWh in FY 2025/26.

The NEA own-generation residual is what remains after those plant rows. The plant tables list no solar plant and none of NEA's isolated small hydro stations.

The subsidiary residual covers the five other operating subsidiary plants listed in Appendix 7: Chilime, Upper Sanjen, Sanjen, Rasuwagadhi and Middle Bhotekoshi, 292.4 MW combined. The reports do not give annual generation for these plants.

> [!finding]
> In FY 2025/26, the three Kulekhani storage plants generated 92.36 GWh, down from 279.79 GWh in FY 2024/25. That is 26% of their 356.45 GWh combined design energy, and their implied plant load factors were 9–11%. The run-of-river and peaking run-of-river plants of 30 MW or more ran at 52–77% implied plant load factor.

> [!finding]
> Plant-level generation covers 5,152 GWh of the 17,454 GWh of domestic supply (NEA, subsidiaries and IPPs) in FY 2025/26, about 30%. The remaining 70% is mostly IPP purchase, which the annual report publishes only as an aggregate.

## Coverage / Method

[`scripts/extract_nea_plant_and_grid_tables.py`](../../../scripts/extract_nea_plant_and_grid_tables.py) builds the CSV from these pages:

- **Generation tables (MWh "Total Actual" column, converted to GWh):** FY 2025/26 report PDF p.186 and FY 2024/25 report PDF p.155. The script checks that the plant rows sum to the printed Grand Total.
- **Installed capacity:** power-plant lists on FY 2025/26 p.185 and FY 2024/25 p.154.
- **Design generation:** FY 2025/26 plant fact-sheets on PDF pp.25–34.
- **Upper Tamakoshi:** FY 2025/26 pp.110–111 and FY 2024/25 p.92. The script checks narrative and fact-sheet constants against the text of their cited pages.

`implied_plf_pct` is generation divided by (MW × 8,760 h). `project_slug` is set only where a page exists under `wiki/pages/entities/`.

## Caveats

- **Design energy is given for FY 2025/26 rows only.** It is a fixed plant parameter taken from the FY 2025/26 fact-sheets, so it is not repeated on the FY 2024/25 rows.
- **Two design figures disagree within the report.** The Generation Directorate summary table on p.187 prints 71.0 GWh for Gandak and 62.7 GWh for Sunkoshi. The fact-sheets give 106.38 and 70 GWh, and the CSV uses the fact-sheet values.
- **Upper Tamakoshi FY 2024/25 has two figures.** The FY 2024/25 report gives 1,529.07 GWh generated. The FY 2025/26 report gives 1,527.77 GWh delivered to NEA for the same year. The CSV keeps the first figure and records the second in its notes.
- **Zero values.** Panauti FY 2025/26 and both thermal plants are recorded as 0 because the table prints "-" in every month.
- **No IPP plant data.** The Urja Khabar audit note in `data/raw/research/` contains only aggregate performance bands, with no plant-level values, so it adds no rows.
