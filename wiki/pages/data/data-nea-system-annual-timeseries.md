# NEA System Annual Time Series FY 2015/16–2025/26

## Summary

This page records eleven fiscal years (FY 2072/73 to FY 2082/83 BS; FY 2015/16 to FY 2025/26 AD) of NEA system totals in one CSV: energy available by supplier, imports and exports, internal sales, national and system peak, system loss, consumer count, sales revenue and profit before tax. FY 2016/17 to FY 2025/26 come from the statistics appendix of [[nea-annual-report-fy2025-26]]. FY 2015/16 comes from the same tables in [[nea-annual-report-fy2024-25]]. Values are copied from the tables. Nothing is interpolated, and cells a table does not report are left blank.

> [!finding]
> Energy available rose from 5,077 GWh in FY 2015/16 to 18,624 GWh in FY 2025/26. Over the same years, purchases from India fell from 35% of the total (1,778 GWh) to 6% (1,171 GWh), and exports rose from 3 GWh to 3,965 GWh.

> [!finding]
> The NEA-subsidiary purchase series jumps from 148 GWh in FY 2020/21 to 1,976 GWh in FY 2021/22. The tables report this as a supplier-category total and do not attribute the step to individual plants.

## Coverage / Method

[`scripts/extract_nea_annual_tables.py`](../../../scripts/extract_nea_annual_tables.py) runs `pdftotext -layout` on fixed appendix pages and parses the labelled ten-year rows. It checks that the capacity components sum to the reported total, prints any differences in the years the two reports share, and prints a cross-check against the monthly energy balance. `--check` fails if the committed CSV is stale. Pages used, as PDF page numbers:

- **FY 2025/26 report:** energy and peaks p.169, sales p.170, consumers p.171, system loss p.173, statement of profit or loss p.156, installed capacity p.185, grid coverage p.13.
- **FY 2024/25 report:** p.138, p.139, p.140, p.142, p.126, p.154 and p.12 for the same series.

Column definitions:
- `energy_sales_gwh` is the "Total Internal Sales (MU)" row, which excludes exports. `energy_export_gwh` is the "Export Sales (MU)" row.
- `nea_revenue_npr_m` is the statement-of-profit-or-loss "Sales Revenue" line.
- `nea_net_profit_npr_m` is "Profit Before Income Tax", which is the bottom line of that table.
- `installed_nea_mw` and `installed_ipp_mw` are sums of the reported hydro, thermal or bagasse, and solar components.
- `electrification_pct` is NEA's reported national grid coverage.

## Caveats

- **Restated FY 2024/25 financials.** The FY 2024/25 report printed provisional figures of NPR 125,277 m revenue and 9,067 m profit before tax. The FY 2025/26 report restates them as 125,741 m and 12,173 m. The CSV uses the later figures. All energy, consumer and loss series agree across the two reports for the years they share.
- **Monthly energy balance uses a different scope.** The FY 2025/26 monthly balance in `data/processed/tables/nea_energy_balance_monthly_fy2025_26.csv` does not sum to these annual totals:

  | Series | Monthly balance (GWh) | Annual table (GWh) | Difference |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | NEA own (RoR/PRoR + storage + solar + thermal) | 2,786 | 2,803 | −17 |
  | NEA subsidiaries | 3,921 | 3,826 | +95 |
  | IPPs | 11,170 | 10,825 | +345 |
  | Imports | 1,155 | 1,171 | −16 |
  | Exports | 3,938 | 3,965 | −27 |
  | System demand vs total available | 19,078 | 18,624 | +454 |

  Treat the two tables as separate measurements and cite each one with its own figure.
- **Capacity figures disagree within the report.** The FY 2025/26 managing director's text gives 3,459 MW across 227 operating IPP projects. The appendix capacity summary on p.185, which the CSV uses, gives 2,710.282 MW for IPPs.
- **System peak is short.** System peak including export load is only reported from FY 2021/22 onward.
- **No older report.** `data/raw/core/nea_annual_report_2020.pdf` is an HTML file, not a PDF, so it could not be used to extend the series further back.
