# Availability-Declaration Penalties

## Summary

**Availability-declaration penalties** are reported contractual deductions that can apply when a hydropower producer delivers less energy than a declared or contracted threshold. The current wiki evidence is secondary PPA analysis. Signed PPAs, project-level deduction records and adjudicated outcomes remain missing.

## Simple Explanation

A generator forecasts how much electricity it expects to supply. Some contracts may permit a deduction when actual delivery falls below the applicable threshold. To determine whether a deduction was valid in a particular case, a reader needs that project's signed contract, dispatch and outage records, invoice calculation and any appeal outcome.

## Reported Standard-Contract Mechanism

[[nea-standard-ppa-clauses]] and [[ad-penalty-clause-research]] report the following formula from secondary analysis of standard wording:

**Compensation = [0.80 × min(Monthly Contract Energy, Availability Declaration) − (Actual Delivered + Scheduled Outage + Forced Outage + Force Majeure Energy)] × Purchase Rate**

This formula has not been checked against a representative set of signed project PPAs. It must not be treated as proof that every negotiated contract contains identical wording or that the provision was enforced in a particular way.

## Curtailment Exposure

The secondary analysis reports no explicit curtailment carve-out in the standard wording it reviewed. That identifies a potential contractual exposure when an off-taker dispatch instruction reduces delivery. It does not establish that every signed PPA lacks relief, that a penalty was imposed after a curtailment instruction, or that a dispute was resolved against a generator.

## Barahi Evidence Status

Legacy pages described [[barahi-hydropower]] as an audited case. The primary audited filing, fiscal year, signed PPA and regulatory or adjudication record are not present. The case is a research lead and must not be used as evidence of a confirmed deduction or legal outcome.

## Evidence Needed

- A representative sample of signed PPAs and amendments.
- Project-level availability declarations, dispatch instructions, accepted energy, outage reasons and invoice calculations.
- Penalty ledgers showing amounts assessed, waived, disputed and recovered.
- ERC, arbitration or court decisions with the applicable contract version.
- A primary company filing for any named project case.

No sector-wide penalty percentage or revenue effect is supported by the current corpus.

## Common Misunderstandings

- **A reported formula is not an enforcement record.** Contract wording and actual deductions are different evidence questions.
- **Low generation does not prove an AD penalty.** Hydrology, outages, maintenance, transmission and dispatch can all affect delivery.
- **A structural conflict risk does not establish a particular legal outcome.** See [[nea-triple-authority]].
- **Barahi is not currently a verified case.** The primary evidence trail is missing.
